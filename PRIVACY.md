# Privacy

Second Sight has no account, no ads and no analytics, and it doesn't use the internet at all: the app has no network permission. Your practices, glimpses, visions, pilgrims and herbs stay on your phone, in the app's own storage (one file). Android's backup may keep a copy, as with any app, if you turned it on.

**Screen time.** To know when the orb mists over, a service counts how long the screen has been on and notices when it turns off. It shows a quiet notification while it runs. Nothing about what you do on the phone is read or kept, only the seconds.

**The light sensor** is read now and then to notice daylight (bright light outdoors) for the tea herbs. Only the minutes in daylight are kept.

**The voice**, if you leave it on, uses the phone's own text-to-speech. Notifications, the bell and vibration are local.

**Links in the settings** (report a bug, suggest an idea, follow updates, the StillCraft chat and the support page) open in your browser or Telegram only when you tap them.
