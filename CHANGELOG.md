## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-23

First release.

- The orb mists with screen time and calls you to look far away; a desk mode mists it by the clock.
- Twelve practices for the eyes and body, with voice, bell and vibration cues, so most can be done with eyes closed.
- Rituals that run several practices in a row, and the apprentice's path: a 21-day course with a gift at the end.
- Six illustrated tales of eight visions each, eight pilgrims with questions and gifts, seasonal tea herbs for time in daylight.
- Home-screen orb widget and a «Darkness» quick-settings tile. English and Russian.
